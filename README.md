# MEASUREME
### Because Weight Isn't a Size.

![MEASUREME Banner](/measureme-spec-type.berrry.app.png)

**MEASUREME** is an AI-assisted fashion-tech prototype developed for the **She Builds Hackathon – Women in Tech**, organized by La French Tech Indonesia.

## The Problem

Finding clothes that fit can be challenging, especially for women with fuller body types.

A blouse might fit perfectly around the bust but feel too tight around the arms. Trousers might fit the waist but feel restrictive around the thighs.

Most size recommendations rely on standard measurements and sometimes body weight, without considering all individual body proportions.

This idea came from my own shopping experiences, which I also found shared by my sisters and friends.

## The Idea

MEASUREME explores a more personalized way to understand clothing fit by connecting individual body measurements with garment measurements.

Instead of relying only on weight or generic size labels, users can visualize their body proportions and explore how clothing may fit them.

## Key Features

- **Body Profile:** Enter personal measurements, including bust, waist, hips, shoulders, and upper arms.
- **3D Body Visualizer:** Explore a visual representation of body proportions.
- **Digital Wardrobe:** Import and organize clothing items.
- **Outfit Generator:** Explore outfit combinations based on occasion, style, and weather.
- **Fit Check:** View estimated garment compatibility based on body and clothing measurements.

## Prototype Limitations

MEASUREME is currently an early-stage prototype built with Berrry.app. The 3D body and garment visualization still need refinement, particularly in body proportions, clothing shape, and realistic fabric behavior.

Further development would focus on improving the 3D rendering, adding camera-assisted measurements, and connecting the wardrobe with real online store size charts.
